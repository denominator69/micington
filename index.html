About the channel

Micington follows the 25ft Bertram Project: stripping, fairing, glassing, wiring and rebuilding Denominator in the shed. Each "blob" is an episode — the wins, the dust, the mistakes and the "idiot continues to rebuild boat" moments. Roofs, bow thrusters, engines, electronics — it all gets done here.

The Story So Far

It started with a mate telling me there was a Bertram 25 for sale just down the road. The plan was to go halves in it, and in everything else. Then he heard what I wanted to do to it. He's a realist, so he bailed. He knew it would take forever and it wasn't what he wanted anyway. He wanted outboards. I wanted an inboard. Smartest bloke in this whole story, honestly.

On 12 October 2018 I bought it anyway, on my own. The plan was to replace a little section of floor. Simple. Weekend job. What could possibly go wrong?

Being straight for a second: I was going through a hard separation at the time (hard for me, anyway), and I needed something to keep my mind off it. This boat did that. If you're a bloke going through the same thing, I genuinely recommend a project like this. It definitely took my mind off it.

Turns out most of the floor had divorced the stringer tops years ago and nobody told me. So naturally the only sensible response was to go "all the way". Floor out, stringers turned into water tanks, fibreglass fuel tanks, deck off, roof off, and the boat lifted off the shed floor onto a trailer. You know, a light tidy-up.

The deadline was Christmas 2020. I missed it. Comfortably. Then I decided the hardtop should pop up on actuators, because a normal roof is for quitters. Then came hand-made curved windows, cold-formed Perspex, fibreglass window frames, a Riva-style dash, a sterndrive refit and a roof that took roughly as long as some marriages last. Each "blob" got me two steps backwards and one step forward, which by my maths means it's finished any day now.

Why do all this? Plenty of reasons. Stubbornness, mostly, plus a deep love of sanding. But one of the big ones, from day one in 2018, was the dashboard: my own boat GUI running on a Raspberry Pi, with every gauge, switch and light on one glorious screen. It was never going to look impressive on a tired old floor with a leaky roof. So the boat had to earn it.

Seven-odd years later, Denominator is painted. A Bertram 25 with a MerCruiser 4.2L turbo diesel that does 27 knots, or will once it's near water. And I'm finally up to the bit I was excited about on day one: the GUI. Nothing else could possibly go wrong now.

I hope the weather gets worse for you. 🌧️
